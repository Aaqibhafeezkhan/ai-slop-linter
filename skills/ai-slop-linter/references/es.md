# Spanish tells the linter cannot see

Source: written for this project from general knowledge of Spanish usage; the families follow the English catalog in `en.md`. No third-party Spanish list was copied.

The linter ships no Spanish rule (see `docs/LANGUAGES.md`), so for Spanish text this short list and the families in `en.md` are the check. Remove what you find by judgement; judge the sentence, not the word.

Every fix follows three rules. Add no fact. Change no number. Add no owner: no `nosotros`, `nuestro`, `yo` or `mi` the source does not use. Keep `tú` or `usted` as the source has it.

## Words used as filler

- `robusto`, `fluido`, `sin fisuras`, `integral`, `crucial`, `fundamental`, `clave` (`un aspecto clave`): give the reason or the measure, or cut.
- `potenciar`, `impulsar`, `fomentar`, `aprovechar al máximo`, `desbloquear` (unlock): say what changes.
- `revolucionario`, `innovador`, `de vanguardia`, `un antes y un después`, `un verdadero testimonio de`: say what is new, or state the fact.
- `en el corazón de`, `vibrante` (a calque), `sinergia`, `ecosistema`, `panorama`: say what is there, or name the field.

## Stock phrases

- Openers and announcing: `En la era digital`, `En un mundo en constante evolución`, `En el panorama actual`, `Sumérgete en`, `Adentrémonos en`, `En este artículo exploraremos`. Start with the content.
- Transitions: `Es importante destacar que`, `Cabe señalar que`, `Cabe destacar que`, `Además,` and `Asimismo,` stacked. State the point.
- Contrast against nobody: `no solo ... sino también` when the first half is not in doubt, `No se trata de X, sino de Y`. Say the second half directly.
- Reveals: `¿El resultado?`, `La clave:`, `¿Por qué? Porque`. Write the plain sentence.
- Inflation: `juega un papel crucial`, `juega un papel fundamental`, `marca un hito`. Say what it does.
- Closers and residue: `En resumen`, `En conclusión`, `El futuro es prometedor`, `No dudes en`, `¡Claro!`, `Espero que esto te ayude`. End on the last fact; delete the wrapper.

## Leave these alone

`¿` and `¡`, the dash that opens dialogue, `«»` quotes, the decimal comma and Spanish number formats are correct. Do not reformat a number: `--compare` counts it as a change. Title Case headings are not Spanish: capitalise the first word and proper nouns only.
