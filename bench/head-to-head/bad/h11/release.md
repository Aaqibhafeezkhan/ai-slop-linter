# Release Notes For Harbor 2.4

Great question! Here's a quick overview of what changed in Harbor 2.4 — and why it matters.

Harbor 2.4 isn't just an update, it's a complete reimagining of how teams ship. Released on 3 March 2026, it stands as a testament to our commitment to seamless, robust tooling, marking a pivotal moment in the evolving landscape of deployment.

- **Speed:** Builds now finish in 41 seconds instead of 95.
- **Caching:** The cache moved from disk to Redis, ensuring teams stay aligned.
- **Support:** Windows ARM64 is supported for the first time 🚀

It's important to note that the old `harbor push --legacy` flag was removed. Experts argue that this change will streamline workflows and empower developers to delve into more meaningful work, which could potentially, arguably, reduce the number of failed deploys by a meaningful amount across a wide variety of teams and organizations everywhere.

No setup. No config. Just results.

In conclusion, the future looks bright for Harbor. I hope this helps! Let me know if you'd like me to expand on any section.
