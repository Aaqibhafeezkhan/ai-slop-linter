I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and by the time I'd checked in I already liked the place.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs let me know about it. The views from the top of each climb mostly made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other people doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any one sight. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels less made for visitors, and it's easier to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history I'd say it's worth the wait.
