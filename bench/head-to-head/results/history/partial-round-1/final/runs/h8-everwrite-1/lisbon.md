I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in the historic Alfama district, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But the climbs pay off, because the views from the top are wide and worth the effort.

You have to ride Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The food is great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight, though. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more lived-in. It made me slow down.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.
