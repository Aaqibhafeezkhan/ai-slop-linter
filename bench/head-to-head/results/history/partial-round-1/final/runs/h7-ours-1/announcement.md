Shared drafts are here. For months our own team was drowning in files named final_v7.docx, and we knew there had to be a better way. Now two people can edit the same doc at once, with every change appearing live for both of them. It's a whole new way to collaborate. Comments stay anchored to the exact sentence they reference, even as the text around them evolves. It's available today on every plan, completely free.
