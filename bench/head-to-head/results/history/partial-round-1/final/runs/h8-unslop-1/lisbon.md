I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, the old district, and I liked the city from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. With all those stairs between the hotel and everything else, my legs felt it. But the views from the top of each climb made it worth it.

You have to ride Tram 28. The trip takes roughly forty minutes, and you'll share it with plenty of other travelers who want the same photos. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national favorite, but my best one came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights, though. Walk a block or two up from the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more local. It's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history, or are just curious, it's worth the wait.
