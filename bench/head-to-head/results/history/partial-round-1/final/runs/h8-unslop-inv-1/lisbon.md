I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel sat high up in the Alfama, and from the moment I checked in I could tell this was a city I'd like.

The hills are as steep as people say. With all those stairs between my hotel and everything else, my legs felt it. But every climb ends in a view over the city, and that made up for the effort.

You should ride Tram 28, a trip of roughly forty minutes that you'll share with plenty of other travelers. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national treasure, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any one sight. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more local, and it's easier to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth the wait if you like history.
