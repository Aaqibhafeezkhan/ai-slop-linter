I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up high in Alfama, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares, where it gets quieter and feels less like a tourist spot. It made me slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.
