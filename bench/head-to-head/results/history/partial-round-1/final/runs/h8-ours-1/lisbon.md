I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in the old Alfama district, and I knew by the time I checked in that I'd like the place.

The hills are as tough as people say. There were stairs everywhere between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But each climb ends in a panoramic view, so I didn't mind much.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes and you'll share it with a lot of other travelers who want the same photos. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the national favorite, but the best one I had came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights, though. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more real, and you slow down without trying.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing, with views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history or are just curious, it's worth the wait.
