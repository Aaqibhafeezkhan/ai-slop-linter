I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any one sight. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and you can slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.
