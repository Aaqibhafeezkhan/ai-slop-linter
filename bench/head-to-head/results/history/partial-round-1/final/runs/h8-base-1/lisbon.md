I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up a hill in Alfama, and I knew pretty quickly that I'd like the place.

The hills are hard work. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything else, and my legs let me know about it. But the views from the top are great, and I thought each climb was worth it.

You have to ride Tram 28, though it's about forty minutes and you'll be sharing it with a lot of other tourists doing the same thing. The food was good too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good, but my favorite was from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any one sight. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place where people actually live, and you end up slowing down.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history I'd say it's worth the wait.
