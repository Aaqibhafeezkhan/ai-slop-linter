## Departures from the frozen protocol and everything that went wrong

1. The session limit stopped the runs twice (14:30 and 19:30 Istanbul): 236 public and 160 held-out attempts failed with `usage_limit_reached`, were kept as `.failed.jsonl` and never scored; the same commands re-ran them. A scan of all 762 transcripts of the measurement (cells, F9, F6) found no result event with an error and no missing result; the coordinator's warning that another controller's `pkill -f sub-shim/claude` could have killed wrapper processes was checked the same way.
2. Public (parallelism 3) and held-out g (parallelism 2) ran at the same time. The held-out g, F9 and F6 runs used the later commit `d902627e405f` or `9c30764ff152` because `prepare-code.sh` refuses a working tree whose `bench/head-to-head` differs from the commit; the product files are byte-identical (`git diff a498189eb8ba HEAD -- src skills hooks scripts` is empty), `package.json` differs only in its `test` script list.
3. The F6-grader loop died once at 132 of 165 votes (probably the `pkill` above); it was started again, and for a while two copies wrote vote files (`mv` errors in the log, 220 grader calls for 165 votes). All 165 vote files are valid; no vote was dropped by hand.
4. The blind-grader prompt asks for the reason before the verdict and runs five votes (owner decision of 2026-10-23); earlier rounds used one vote. Blind grading of F9 and F6-runs was not done; their totals use the scripted criteria only (public maximum 57).
5. The `ours` transcripts carry more than one `result` event in some cells; cost per cell uses the last one.
6. The set g result is saturated. The frozen rule was not changed after seeing it.
7. The development gate (`devgate/`) is separate and enters no table here.
8. Not done: nothing of the frozen protocol was skipped; the F9 transcripts are published scrubbed in `f9/`, the F9 and F6-runs re-runs were not blind-graded (see 4).
