I spent five days in Lisbon last October and it won me over. My hotel was up a hill in Alfama, and I knew from the moment I checked in that this was a special place.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. Stairs separated the hotel from pretty much everything, and my legs felt it. The views from the top were good enough that I kept climbing anyway.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. For pastéis de nata I went to Pastéis de Belém, which deserves its fame, but my favorite came from a small bakery in Graça. They were still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels less touristy. It made me want to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue was long, but I'd wait again if you like history.

I'd go back, probably in spring, and with better shoes.
