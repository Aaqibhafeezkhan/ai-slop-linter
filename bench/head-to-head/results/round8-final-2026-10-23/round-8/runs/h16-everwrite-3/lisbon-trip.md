I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs let me know about it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. For food, the pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason. My favorite, though, came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels less like a tourist stop.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long. I'd still go if you like history or just want the view.

I'd go back, in spring and with better shoes.
