I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in the historic Alfama district, and from the moment I checked in I knew this was a special place.

The famous hills are hard work. There were countless stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt it. But every climb ends in a panoramic view, so it was worth it.

You have to ride Tram 28, a roughly forty-minute journey shared with plenty of other travelers who want the same photos. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national treasure, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feel of the city. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter and less touristy, and you slow down.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you like history or are just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
