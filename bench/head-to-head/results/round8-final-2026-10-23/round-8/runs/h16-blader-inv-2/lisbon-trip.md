I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels more local, and it's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth it for me, and it's a good stop if you like history.

I'd go back, next time in spring and with better shoes.
