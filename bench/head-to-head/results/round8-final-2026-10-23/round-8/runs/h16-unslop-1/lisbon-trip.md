I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in the Alfama district, and I knew by the time I'd checked in that I liked the place.

The hills are as steep as people say. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them, but the views at the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes roughly forty minutes and was packed with other travelers who had the same idea. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous name, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like the city people actually live in. São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth it, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
