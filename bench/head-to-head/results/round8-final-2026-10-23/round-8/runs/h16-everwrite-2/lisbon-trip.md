I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I knew by the time I'd checked in that I'd like the place.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But every climb ended in a view, so I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, and they're good. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place people live. It's an easy place to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history, it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
