I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and getting to anything else meant a lot of stairs, which my legs did not enjoy. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous and they are good, but my favorite came from a small bakery in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels less like a tourist stop. I liked slowing down there.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth the wait if you like history.

I would go back, next time in spring, with better shoes.
