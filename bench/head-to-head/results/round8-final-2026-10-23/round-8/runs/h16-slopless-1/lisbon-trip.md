I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, on one of the highest streets, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are hard. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt it. But the views from the top of each climb were good enough that I didn't mind much.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for a reason, but my favorite was from a small bakery in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place where people actually live, and I ended up slowing down without trying to.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queue can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
