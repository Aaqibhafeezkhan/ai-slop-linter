I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and there were stairs between it and pretty much everything else. My legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb were good enough that I stopped complaining about halfway through.

I rode Tram 28, of course. It takes about forty minutes and it's packed with other people doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for a reason, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was how different the city felt just a block or two above the main squares. It got quieter, and I ended up walking slower.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queue was long. I'd still wait again if you care about history at all.

I'd go back, in spring, with better shoes.
