I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, which sounded charming when I booked it and turned out to be a lot of stairs.

The hills are steep, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels less like a tourist city. São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
