I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, on a hill, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are steep. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs let me know about it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was how different the city felt just a block or two above the main squares. It was quieter there, and I ended up slowing down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
