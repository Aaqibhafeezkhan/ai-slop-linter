I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was high up in the historic Alfama district.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb were good enough that I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two up from the main squares and it gets quieter. That's where the city felt most like somewhere people actually live, and I ended up slowing down there.

São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history I'd still go.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
