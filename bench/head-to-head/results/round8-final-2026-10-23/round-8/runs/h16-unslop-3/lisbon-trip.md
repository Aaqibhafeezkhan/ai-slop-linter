I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it a lot. My hotel was high up in Alfama, an old district, and I was charmed by it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. Getting from the hotel to anywhere else meant a lot of stairs, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other people who want the same photo. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place where people live. I ended up slowing down there without meaning to.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, and I'd still wait again, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
