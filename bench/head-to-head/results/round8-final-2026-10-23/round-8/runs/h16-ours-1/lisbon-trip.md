I spent five days in Lisbon last October and it completely won me over. I knew it from the moment I checked into my hotel, high up in the Alfama.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were countless stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs certainly felt it. But every climb ends in a panoramic view, so it's worth it.

Ride Tram 28, a roughly forty-minute journey shared with plenty of fellow travelers. The food is great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national treasure, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feeling of the place. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter and feels more local, and you slow down.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history or are just curious, it's worth it.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
