I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it a lot. My hotel was high up in Alfama, which meant stairs between it and everything else. My legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, about forty minutes, with plenty of other tourists on board. For pastéis de nata I went to Pastéis de Belém, which everyone raves about, but the best one I had came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two up from the main squares it gets quieter, and you can slow down there. São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
