I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel sat high up in Alfama, the old district, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

Take Tram 28; it's a ride of roughly forty minutes, and you'll share it with plenty of other travelers. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous address, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter, which is a good reason to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth the wait, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring this time and with better shoes.
