I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it more than I expected to. My hotel was way up in Alfama, and I knew by the first evening that I'd picked a good neighborhood.

The hills are real. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything else, and my legs complained the whole week. But nearly every climb ended in a view, so I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other people doing the same thing. For food, the pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones and they're good. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked best was walking a block or two above the main squares. It gets quiet fast, and nobody seemed to be in a hurry.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue was long, but I'd wait again.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
