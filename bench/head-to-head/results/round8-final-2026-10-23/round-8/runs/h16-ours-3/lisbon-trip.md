I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was high up in the Alfama district, and from the moment I checked in I could tell the trip would be good.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. With all those stairs between my hotel and everything else, my legs felt it. Every climb ends in a big view over the city, though, so it was worth it.

I rode Tram 28, a roughly forty-minute journey shared with plenty of other travelers. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more lived in, and it's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I'd say it's worth it if you like history or are just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
