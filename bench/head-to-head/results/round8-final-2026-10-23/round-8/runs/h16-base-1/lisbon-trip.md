I spent five days in Lisbon last October. My hotel was up in Alfama, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top were good enough that I didn't mind much.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels less touristy. I liked that part best.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue can be long, but I thought it was worth it.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
