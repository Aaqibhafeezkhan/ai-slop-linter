I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I knew I'd like the city before I had even unpacked.

The hills are real. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb are good enough that I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes. It was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, and they're good, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares. It gets quieter up there, and it feels more like the real city.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long. I'd still go, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
