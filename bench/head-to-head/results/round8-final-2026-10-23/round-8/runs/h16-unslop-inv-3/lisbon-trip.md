I spent five days in Lisbon last October, staying in a hotel high up in the Alfama district.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and just about everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top make up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes roughly forty minutes and was full of other travelers doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a little-known place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

A block or two above the main squares the city gets quieter and feels more local. São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
