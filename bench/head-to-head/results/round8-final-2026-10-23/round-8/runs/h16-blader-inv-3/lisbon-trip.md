I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I knew pretty quickly I'd like the place.

The hills are hard. There were stairs everywhere between the hotel and anything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top are great, though, and I thought they were worth it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists. The food was good too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feeling of the city. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels less touristy, and I ended up slowing down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
