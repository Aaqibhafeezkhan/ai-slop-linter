I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, and I knew pretty quickly that I'd like the place.

The hills are no joke. There were stairs everywhere between the hotel and anything else, and my legs felt it. But the views from the top were great, so I didn't really mind.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares. It gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there, and it made me want to slow down.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes. Next time in spring, and with better shoes.
