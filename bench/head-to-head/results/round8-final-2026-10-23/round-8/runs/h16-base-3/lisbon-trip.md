I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, and I liked the place the moment I checked in.

The hills are no joke. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs let me know about it. But the views from the top of each climb made up for it.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes about forty minutes, and it's packed with other tourists who all want the same photos. The food was great too. Everyone goes to Pastéis de Belém for pastéis de nata, and they're good, but my favorite was from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was a feeling more than any one sight. Walk a block or two up from the main squares and it gets quieter and feels more like real life there. It made me want to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue can be long, but I thought it was worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes. Next time in spring, and with better shoes.
