I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in the Alfama, and I knew almost right away that I'd like the city.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was a block or two above the main squares, where it gets quieter and you can slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth it for me.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
