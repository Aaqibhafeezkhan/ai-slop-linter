I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was high up in the historic Alfama district, and from the moment I checked in I knew this was a special place.

Yes, the hills are hard. There were countless stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But every climb ends in a panoramic view, which made it worth it.

Ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and you'll share it with plenty of other travelers who want the same thing. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national favorite, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feeling of the city. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter, with a side of Lisbon that invites you to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you like history or are just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes. I'd happily fall for Lisbon all over again.
