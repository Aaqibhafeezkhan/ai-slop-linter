I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are real. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top are good enough that I didn't mind climbing.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for a reason, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights, though. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there. I ended up slowing down without trying to.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing, with a great view over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes. Next time in spring, and with better shoes.
