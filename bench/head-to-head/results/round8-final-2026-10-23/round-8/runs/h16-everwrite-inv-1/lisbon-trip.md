I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it a lot. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I was sold the first time I walked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs let me know. But the views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels less like it's performing for visitors. I ended up slowing down without meaning to.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
