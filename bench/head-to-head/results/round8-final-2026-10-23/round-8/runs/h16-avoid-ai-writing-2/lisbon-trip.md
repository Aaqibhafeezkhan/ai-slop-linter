I spent five days in Lisbon last October and the city completely stole my heart. From the moment I checked into my hotel high up in Alfama, I knew I was somewhere special.

The famous hills are hard work. There were countless stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs certainly felt it! But every climb ends in a panoramic view, and that makes it worth it.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and plenty of other travelers had the same idea. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national treasure, but my favorite came from a spot in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Wander a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more authentic. It makes you slow down.

São Jorge Castle is a must-see for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you're into history or just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes! Lisbon is the kind of place you fall for again and again.
