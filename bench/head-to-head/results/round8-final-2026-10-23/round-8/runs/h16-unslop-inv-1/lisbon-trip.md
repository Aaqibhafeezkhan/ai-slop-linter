I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it more than I expected. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I was charmed by it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, about forty minutes, shared with a lot of other travelers. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels less like it's put on for visitors. I slowed down there without meaning to.

São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops. The queues can be long, and I'd still wait again, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
