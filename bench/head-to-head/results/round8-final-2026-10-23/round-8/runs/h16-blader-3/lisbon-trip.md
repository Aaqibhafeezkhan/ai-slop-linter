I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and the hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs let me know about it. The views from the top of each climb were good enough that I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other people doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place people live. That was the part I liked most.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth the wait.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
