I spent five days in Lisbon last October and the city stole my heart. From the moment I checked into my charming hotel, high up in the Alfama district, I knew I was somewhere special.

Yes, the famous hills are challenging. There were countless stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs certainly felt it! But every climb rewards you with panoramic views that make it all worthwhile.

You have to ride Tram 28, a roughly forty-minute journey shared with plenty of fellow travelers. The food was divine too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a beloved national treasure, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Lisbon is special for the sights, but more for the feeling. Wander a block or two above the main squares and you'll find a quieter, more authentic side of the city that invites you to slow down.

São Jorge Castle is a must-see, with great views over the rooftops below. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you're into history or just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes! Lisbon is a place to fall in love with again and again.
