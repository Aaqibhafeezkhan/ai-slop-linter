I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it more than I expected to. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I knew I'd picked a good spot as soon as I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. Stairs stood between the hotel and nearly everything else, and my legs felt it by day two. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares. The streets get quieter there and feel more lived-in, and I ended up slowing down without meaning to.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue was long, but I'd wait again, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, probably in spring, and I'd bring better shoes.
