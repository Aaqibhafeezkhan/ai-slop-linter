I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, so there were stairs between it and everything else, and my legs noticed. The hills are hard work, but each climb ends with a view that makes up for it.

You have to ride Tram 28, though it takes about forty minutes and you share it with plenty of other travelers. The food was great. Everyone goes to Pastéis de Belém for pastéis de nata, and they're worth it, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter, which makes it easier to slow down. São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

I'd go back, next time in spring and with better shoes.
