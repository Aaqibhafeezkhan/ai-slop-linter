I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, the old district, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two up from the main squares and the city gets quieter. That's where I felt like I could slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
