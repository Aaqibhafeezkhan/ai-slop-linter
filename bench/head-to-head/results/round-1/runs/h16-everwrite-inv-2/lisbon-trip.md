I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, on one of the hills, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter, and the city feels less like it's put on for visitors. I slowed down there without meaning to.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queue was long, but I'd say it's worth it if you like history or are just curious.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
