I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in the Alfama, and by the time I'd checked in I knew I liked the place.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But each climb ends with a view over the city, so I stopped minding.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and plenty of other travelers had the same idea. As for food, the pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter and feels more like a real city, the kind of place that makes you slow down.

São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history or are just curious, it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
