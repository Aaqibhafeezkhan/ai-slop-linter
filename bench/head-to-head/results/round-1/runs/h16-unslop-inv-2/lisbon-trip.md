I spent five days in Lisbon last October and the city won me over. My hotel was high up in the old Alfama district, with a lot of stairs between it and everything else, and my legs felt every one. The views at the top of each climb made up for it.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and you share it with plenty of other travelers. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Lisbon is also about more than the sights. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter and feels more authentic, a part of the city that lets you slow down.

São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
