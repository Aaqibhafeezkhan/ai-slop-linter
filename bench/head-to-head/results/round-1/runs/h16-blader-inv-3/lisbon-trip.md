I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. I stayed in a hotel high up in the Alfama, the old district, and by the time I'd checked in I already liked the place.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and just about everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes roughly forty minutes and was packed with other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a place in Graça that most visitors would walk past, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any single sight. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels more local, and it's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
