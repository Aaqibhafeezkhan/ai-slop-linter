I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it a lot. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and the hills are as steep as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything else, and my legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça I stumbled on, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two up from the main squares and it gets quieter. That's where the city felt most like a place people actually live.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth the wait, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
