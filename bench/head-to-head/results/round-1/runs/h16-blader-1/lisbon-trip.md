I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is full of other tourists doing the same thing. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous place, but my favorite came from a small bakery in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels less like a tourist spot. I'd recommend doing that and slowing down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
