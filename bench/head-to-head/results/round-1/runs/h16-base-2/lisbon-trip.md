I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, on one of the old hilly streets, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are hard work. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs let me know about it. But the views from the top are great, so I didn't mind much.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous and they're good, but my favorite was from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the big sights. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there, and it's easy to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth it for me, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes. Next time in spring, and with better shoes.
