I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are hard. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small bakery in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two up from the main squares and the city gets quieter. That was the part I liked most, because it made me slow down.

I also went to São Jorge Castle, which has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
