I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in the Alfama district, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But each climb ends with a view over the city, so it's worth it.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and you'll share it with plenty of other travelers who had the same idea. The food was great too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous across the country, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter and feels more real, and you end up slowing down.

São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops, and you should see it. The queues can be long, but if you like history or are just curious, it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
