I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, which sounds romantic until you count the stairs between it and everything else. My legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, about forty minutes, packed with other people who had the same idea. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite was from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares. It gets quieter up there and feels less like it's put on for visitors. It's a good place to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queue was long, but if you like history I'd say it's worth the wait.

I'd go back, in spring this time, and with better shoes.
