I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything else, and my legs felt it. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other people doing the same thing. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feel of the city. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, it gets quieter and you can slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth it, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
