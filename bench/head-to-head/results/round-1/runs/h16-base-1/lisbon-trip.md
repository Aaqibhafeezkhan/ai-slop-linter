I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place right away.

The hills are hard work. There were stairs between the hotel and just about everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top are great, and I thought they made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous, and they're good. My favorite one came from a small place in Graça, though, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't any one sight. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there. It made me slow down.

I also went to São Jorge Castle. The views over the rooftops are good. The queue can be long, but it was worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
