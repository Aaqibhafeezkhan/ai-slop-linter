I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, which sounded charming when I booked it. It was, but there were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs noticed. The hills are as steep as people say. The views from the top are good enough that I mostly didn't mind.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous place. My favorite, though, was from a little bakery in Graça, still warm from the oven.

If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like a place people actually live. I liked slowing down there.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, and I'd still go back for it, especially if you like history.

Would I return? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
