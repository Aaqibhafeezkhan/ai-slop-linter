I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it more than I expected. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I knew within minutes of checking in that I'd be happy there.

The hills are as bad as everyone says. There were stairs between my hotel and just about everything, and my legs let me know. The views from the top of each climb made up for it, though.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other people doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares, where it gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there. It's easy to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history I'd still go.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
