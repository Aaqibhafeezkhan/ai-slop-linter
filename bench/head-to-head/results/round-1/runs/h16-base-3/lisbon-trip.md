I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked it from the minute I checked in.

The hills are hard work. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything, and my legs noticed. But the views from the top of each climb were worth it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other tourists doing the same thing. The food was great too. Everyone goes to Pastéis de Belém for pastéis de nata, and they're good, but my favorite was from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't the sights, though. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, it gets quieter and feels more like people actually live there. I ended up slowing down without trying to.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth it, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
