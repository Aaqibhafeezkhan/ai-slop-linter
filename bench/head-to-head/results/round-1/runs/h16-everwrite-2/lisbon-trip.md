I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was up in Alfama, high on the hill, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs noticed. But the views at the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other people doing the same thing. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous one. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. If you walk a block or two above the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels more lived in, and it's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought it was worth the wait, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes. Next time in spring, and with better shoes.
