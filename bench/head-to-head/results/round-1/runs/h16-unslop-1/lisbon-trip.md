I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it more than I expected. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I was charmed by it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as steep as everyone says. There were stairs between the hotel and pretty much everything else, and my legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists with the same idea. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares. It gets quieter there, and the city felt less staged.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it was worth the wait, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
