I spent five days in Lisbon last October and liked it a lot. My hotel was high up in Alfama.

The hills are hard work. There were stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs noticed. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is shared with plenty of other tourists. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was walking a block or two above the main squares, where the city gets quieter and feels less like a postcard.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring, and with better shoes.
