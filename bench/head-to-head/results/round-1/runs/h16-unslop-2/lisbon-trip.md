I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked it from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as hard as everyone says. There were countless stairs between the hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top of each climb made up for it.

You have to ride Tram 28. It takes roughly forty minutes, and plenty of other travelers had the same idea. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a national treasure, but my favorite came from a hidden gem in Graça, still warm from the oven.

Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter, and it's easier to slow down.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but it's worth it if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
