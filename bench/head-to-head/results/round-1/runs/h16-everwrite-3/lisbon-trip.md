I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the moment I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and just about everything else, and my legs complained. But the views at the top of each climb were good enough that I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and is packed with other tourists doing the same thing. For pastéis de nata, Pastéis de Belém is the famous stop. My favorite, though, came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. If you walk a block or two up from the main squares, the city gets quieter and feels more lived in, and it's easy to slow down there.

São Jorge Castle has great views over the rooftops. The queues can be long. I thought it was worth the wait, especially if you like history.

I'd go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
