I spent five days in Lisbon last October and loved it. My hotel was high up in Alfama, and I liked the place from the minute I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between the hotel and just about everything, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top of each climb were good enough that I stopped minding.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was packed with other people doing the same thing. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for a reason, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most wasn't a sight. Walk a block or two above the main squares and the city gets quieter, and you can slow down.

São Jorge Castle is worth seeing for the views over the rooftops. The queues can be long. I'd still say go, especially if you like history.

Would I go back? Yes, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
