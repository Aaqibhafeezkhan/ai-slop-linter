I spent five days in Lisbon last October and I loved it. My hotel was up in the old Alfama district, and I knew I'd like the place as soon as I checked in.

The hills are as bad as people say. There were stairs between my hotel and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. But each climb ends with a view over the city, and that made up for it.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other travelers who wanted the same photos. The food was great. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are famous for good reason, but my favorite came from a little place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most was the feel of the city. Walk a block or two above the main squares and it gets quieter and less touristy, and you end up slowing down.

São Jorge Castle looks out over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but if you like history it's worth the wait.

Would I go back? Yes, next time in spring, and with better shoes.
