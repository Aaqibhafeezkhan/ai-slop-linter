I spent five days in Lisbon last October. My hotel was up in the Alfama district, with a lot of stairs between it and everything else, and my legs felt every one of them. The views from the top were worth the climb.

Everyone tells you to ride Tram 28, so I did. The ride took about forty minutes, and most of the car was other tourists taking pictures out of the windows. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

The part of Lisbon I liked best starts a block or two above the main squares, where the streets go quiet and nobody is in a hurry. São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops and a long queue to get them. The queues can be long, but I thought the visit was worth it for the history.
