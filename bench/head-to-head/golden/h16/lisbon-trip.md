I spent five days in Lisbon last October. My hotel was in the Alfama district, up a lot of stairs from everything else, and my legs noticed. The views from the top were worth the climb.

I rode Tram 28, which takes about forty minutes and was full of other tourists. The food was good too. The pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are the famous ones, but my favorite came from a small place in Graça, still warm from the oven.

What I liked most starts a block or two above the main squares. The streets go quiet there and nobody is in a hurry.

São Jorge Castle has good views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I thought the visit was worth it for the history.

I would go back, in spring next time, and with better shoes.
